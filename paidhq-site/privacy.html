Privacy policy

Last updated July 25, 2026

This policy covers PaidHQ and every product in the PaidHQ family, including BudgetHQ (together, the "Services"), operated by Fractional Paid Media ("PaidHQ," "we," "us"). It explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn, or Facebook, the basic profile information those providers share (typically name and email) — used only to authenticate you, not to pull advertising data.

Workspace data. The budgets, tags, dimensions, saved views, and configuration you create inside a Service. This is yours — we store it to run the product, not to use for anything else.

Advertising spend data. If you connect an ad account (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Microsoft Advertising, and others as they're added) or upload a file/CSV/spreadsheet export, we store the campaign, ad set, spend, impressions, and click data needed to power budgeting and pacing. See "Connecting ad accounts" below for exactly what access this involves.

Files you upload. Reporting files, CSV exports, or other documents you add to a workspace.

AI feature usage. If you use an AI-assisted feature (like BudgetHQ's Ask AI), your prompts and the relevant workspace data needed to answer them are sent to our AI provider (currently Anthropic) to generate a response, and your conversation history is stored so you can return to it.

Support and communications. Anything you send us directly — support requests, feedback, replies to emails we send.

2. Connecting ad accounts

When you connect an ad platform to a Service, you're sent through that platform's own OAuth consent screen, and you can revoke access at any time from that platform's own account settings, or by disconnecting inside the Service.

We only ever read your reporting data — we never create, edit, pause, launch, or delete campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets, or bids on any connected platform, and we do not manage bidding. Some platforms' permission scopes are broader than the specific actions we perform (for example, a scope that technically allows account management) — we request the narrowest scope each platform's own API makes available for reporting access, but where a platform bundles reporting and management permissions together, our actual usage is still limited strictly to reading spend, impressions, and click data for the budgeting and pacing features described on this site.

3. How we use what we collect

  • To operate the Services — computing budgets, pacing, forecasts, and reports from your own data.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account and workspace.
  • To respond to support requests and improve the Services based on how they're actually used.
  • To send you service-related communications (for example, a sync failing, or a change to these terms). We don't send marketing email you haven't asked for.

We do not sell your personal data or your workspace's advertising data to anyone, for any purpose.

4. Who we share data with

We use a small number of subprocessors to run the Services — each only processes data as needed to provide their specific piece of the infrastructure, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Neon — our database host (Postgres), where workspace, budget, and spend data lives.
  • Vercel — application hosting and the serverless functions that run the Services.
  • Supabase — authentication (login/session management).
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (for example, emailing you a report you asked to export).
  • Anthropic — powers AI-assisted features (like Ask AI); relevant workspace data is sent as context to generate a response when you use one of these features.

We don't share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else for marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of PaidHQ or our users.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account or workspace is active. You can delete a workspace or your account from within the Service, which removes the associated data from our systems (subject to reasonable backup retention windows before final deletion completes). If you'd rather we handle it directly, email privacy@paidhq.io.

6. Cookies and similar technology

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (like your last-used workspace or theme). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit, access controls, and reputable infrastructure providers — to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but protecting your data is a first-order concern in how we build.

8. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for business use by professionals and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

9. International users

Our infrastructure providers may process and store data in the United States and other countries. By using the Services, you consent to that transfer.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change, and update the date at the top. Material changes affecting how your data is used will be communicated directly where required.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data — privacy@paidhq.io.
