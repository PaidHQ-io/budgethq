◆ budgethq — the first room

Real budget pacing, not a flat average pretending to forecast.

Most budget tools divide spend-to-date by days elapsed and call it a forecast. BudgetHQ was built by someone who actually manages paid media budgets for a living — every feature exists because a real spreadsheet couldn't do it well enough.

Forecasting that adapts

Auto blends your long-run rate with the last 7 days, leaning on whichever is more trustworthy as they diverge — no flat cumulative average diluting a real budget change for weeks.

Every platform, one pacing view

Native sync with Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and Microsoft Advertising, plus CSV and spreadsheet import for everything else — one number instead of six tabs.

Segmented your way

Budget by product, region, funnel stage, or any dimension your business actually runs on — not just the platform's own campaign structure.

Built for real budget shapes

Committed lump-sum spend, seasonal spikes, day-of-week seasonality — handled directly, instead of reading as false alarms in your pacing status.

◆ how we build

Built deliberately. Not all at once.

It would be easy to ship five thin tools in a rush and call it a platform. We're doing this in order instead — get one tool genuinely right, let real people use it, then build the next. BudgetHQ is the first room in the HQ. It won't be the last, but it had to be the first one done properly.
